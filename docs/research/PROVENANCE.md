Source material we do NOT redistribute: the Burnout 3 ISO, SLUS_210.50 ELF, VU
microcode, PS2 BIOS, any SDK or CodeWarrior binaries. The user supplies their
own disc at runtime.

Source material we do NOT use at all (decision 2026-10-12, milestone 4
groundwork): the leaked RenderWare 3.7 source (circulated as
`sigmaco/rwsrc-v3.7.0.2` and relied on by two PS2 decomps above) is never
opened, cloned or cited; RenderWare knowledge comes from the ELF, librw and
public documentation only. The proprietary Metrowerks and SN compiler binaries
packaged for decomp.me never enter this repo or its CI. Library identifications
in `docs/research/decomp-groundwork.md` (newlib-derived libc, Speex, EA
DirtySock, Logitech, RenderWare) rest on strings inside the ELF alone; no source
or documentation of those libraries was consulted, so they have no rows here.
